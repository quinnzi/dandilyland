What is DandilyLand?

Dandilyland is a board game where you roll a die and the die tells you how many spaces to go. Sounds simple, yes? Well, it is structured similarly the game that's popular with children. However, there are different types of tiles you can land on. They can determine whether you can beat the game or not. More about those below. Whether you beat the game is based on a web of chance thaat I myself am going to spin.

Candyland, which this game is sort of inspired by, was a game designed to teach children how to count by rolling die and going the amount that the die said they should go. In the spirit of that, I think I should use as many numbers as possible in this project. Because have I suffered enough? I think not.

SO. In future iterations of the game, you will be given the option to choose the character of Axle, Berry, Can, and Diamond.

  • Axle has STRENGTH, so he is less likely to be affected by STATE or KILL tiles.
  • Berry has CHARM, which gives him much morse of a chance of landing double numbers on the die.
  • Can has SMARTS, which means he gets more of a score boost on quests tiles, and a chance of avoiding a kill tile.
  • Diamond has LUCK, which means there is less of a probability that you will land on bad tiles in general.

In the future this game will be multiplayer, but I can't deal w servers all in one week so uhh, you can choose! Enjoy!
